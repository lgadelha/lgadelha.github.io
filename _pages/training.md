## Research Data Management

### GreenRobust Research Data Management Workshop

**2026** - Centre for Organismal Studies, Heidelberg University

Hands-on workshop for researchers in the Cluster of Excellence GreenRobust, held on June 19 and July 27, 2026.

- Topics: data management planning, data documentation and metadata, FAIR principles, data preservation and discovery, legal and privacy issues
- Hands-on session on structuring research data as [DataPLANT](https://www.nfdi4plants.org/) Annotated Research Contexts (ARCs)
- [Workshop materials](https://bwgit.de/hd-vk355/rdm_workshop) (CC BY 4.0)

### Research Data Management for Biomedical Researchers

**2022** - University of Jena, online

Course for biomedical researchers at all qualification levels, co-taught with Cora Assmann (RDM Helpdesk, University of Jena) together with ZB MED - Information Centre for Life Sciences, November 23-25, 2022.

- Topics: data life cycle, data management plans and tools, data collection, processing, publishing, preservation and reuse, legal aspects and licenses
- [Course page](https://www.microverse-cluster.de/de/events/microverse-course-research-data-management-for-biomedical-researchers.html) · [JSMC listing](https://www.jsmc-phd.de/event/microverse-course-research-data-management-for-biomedical-researchers.html)

### Research Data Management - Make your data count!

**2022** - University of Jena

Workshop co-taught with Cora Assmann and Jitendra Gaikwad, offered through the University of Jena's qualification program, including the "Führung in der Wissenschaft" (Leadership in Science) certificate program. Held twice: April 20-22, 2022, and October 28 and November 1, 2022 (online).

- Topics: basic definitions in research data management and the research data life cycle

### Microverse Research Data Management Workshop: OMERO Tutorial

**2022** - University of Jena

Bring-your-own-data workshop on managing microscopy images with OMERO, co-taught with Aurélie Jost and Patrick Then (Microverse Imaging Center), September 30, 2022.

- Topics: research data management and good scientific practice, the Microverse research data management infrastructure, microscopy data, and the Open Microscopy Environment (OME) and OMERO
- Hands-on session: importing images with OMERO.insight, searching, viewing images with OMERO.iviewer, and annotating images with tags and key-value metadata
- [Event page](https://www.microverse-cluster.de/de/events/data-management-workshop-omero-tutorial.html)

### Bring Your Own Metabolomic Data

**2021** - University of Jena

Bring-your-own-data workshop on sharing metabolomics data for researchers in the Cluster of Excellence Balance of the Microverse, co-taught with Maria Sorokina, June 1, 2021.

- Topics: minimum reporting standards for metabolomics data, the ISA-Tab metadata format, metabolomics and generalist repositories, and publishing data and data papers
- Hands-on session: preparing a submission to the MetaboLights repository

### Microverse Course: Research Data Management

**2021** - University of Jena, online
