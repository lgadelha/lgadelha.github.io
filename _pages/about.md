I support research groups in managing their data and computational workflows following best practices like the FAIR principles. I work as a Data Steward in the [Central Data Hub](https://greenrobust.de/cdh-central-data-hub/) of the Cluster of Excellence [GreenRobust](https://greenrobust.de) at the [Centre for Organismal Studies](https://www.cos.uni-heidelberg.de), [Heidelberg University](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de), and have worked on scientific computing and research data in the life sciences, bioinformatics, and biodiversity informatics since 2004.

**Areas of expertise**

- **Research data management:** data management planning, metadata and ontologies, and FAIR data packaging with DataPLANT ARCs and RO-Crate.
- **Computational workflows:** reproducible and FAIR computational workflows with Nextflow and CWL, including provenance, observability, and scalability.
- **Research data infrastructure:** federated data infrastructures for biodiversity (GBIF IPT), genomic data infrastructure (FAIR Data Point, Beacon, Federated EGA), and the plant sciences (DataPLANT DataHUB).
- **Training:** [workshops and courses](https://lgadelha.github.io/training/) on research data management and FAIR practices for researchers at all career stages.

**Communities**

- [DataPLANT](https://www.nfdi4plants.org/): participating in the [Data Steward Circle](https://www.nfdi4plants.org/events/datasteward-circle/30-09-2026/) and in community events, the project itself is part of the German National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI), providing the tools and standards used in GreenRobust.
- [Research Data Management Community](https://elixir-europe.org/communities/research-data-management) (ELIXIR): currently contributing to the [Data Stewardship Handbook](https://elixir-europe.github.io/ds-handbook/) with guidelines for data stewards on making computational workflows FAIR.
- [FAIR Computational Workflows Working Group](https://workflows.community/groups/fair/), Workflows Community Initiative (WCI): contributed to the discussions and the recently-published [guidelines](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04451-9) on applying the FAIR Principles to computational workflows, will also attend the [Dagstuhl Seminar](https://www.dagstuhl.de/27411) proposed by the working group.

Previously, I coordinated the German node of the European Genomic Data Infrastructure ([GDI](https://gdi.onemilliongenomes.eu/)) at the German Human Genome-Phenome Archive ([GHGA](https://www.ghga.de)), German Cancer Research Center ([DKFZ](https://www.dkfz.de)); managed research data for the Cluster of Excellence [Balance of the Microverse](https://www.microverse-cluster.de/en/) at the [University of Jena](https://www.uni-jena.de); and worked as a Senior Technologist at the National Laboratory for Scientific Computing ([LNCC](https://www.lncc.br)) in Brazil on high-performance scientific workflows and scientific information systems such as [SiBBr](https://sibbr.gov.br/), [SISS-Geo](https://sissgeo.lncc.br/), and [HIHISIV](https://hihisiv.github.io/). 

I hold a D.Sc. in Computer and Systems Engineering from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro ([UFRJ](https://pesc.coppe.ufrj.br/)), with a doctoral internship at the [Computation Institute](https://voices.uchicago.edu/compinst/) of the University of Chicago and Argonne National Laboratory, where I worked on provenance for the [Swift](http://swift-lang.org/main/) parallel scripting language.
